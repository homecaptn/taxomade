A house near Chania that you use in June and let the rest of the summer is a short-term rental from the first booking. You need a registry number, a declaration per stay, a nightly climate fee and a Greek tax return, even if you live in Munich or Manchester. Here is how holiday home rental tax in Greece works in 2026, with the Crete specifics.

## The short answer

- **Registration:** an AMA number in AADE's Short-Term Stay Property Registry for each property or room you let, shown on every listing.
- **Each stay:** a declaration in myAADE by the 20th of the month after the guest leaves.
- **Climate resilience fee:** €8 per night from April to October and €2 from November to March; €15 and €4 for detached houses over 80 m².
- **Tax with one or two properties:** 15% to 45% on the rental income scale, no VAT, annual return with form E2.
- **Three properties or more:** a business with 13% VAT on all of them.
- **Crete:** no freeze on new registrations; Airbnb tax in Crete is the same as anywhere in Greece.
- **At home:** Germany exempts the rent, without raising the rate on other income; the UK taxes it and credits the Greek tax.

Letting long-term instead (60 days or more per tenant)? That is covered in our guide to [rental income tax for foreign owners](/guides/rental-income-tax-greece-foreign-owners/).

## What counts as a short-term rental in Greece

Under AADE's rules, a short-term rental is a furnished property let for **up to 59 days per booking**, with no services beyond bed linen ([AADE FAQ, September 2025](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-09/FAQs_braxixronias_mistosis_0.pdf)). The limit applies per booking, not per year, and it makes no difference whether the guest books through Airbnb or directly. Offer breakfast or cleaning during the stay, and it becomes tourist accommodation, a business from the first property.

## Before the first guest: registering for Airbnb

### AFM and myAADE

You need a Greek tax number (AFM) and myAADE credentials; most owners have them from the purchase. See [AFM for property buyers](/guides/afm-greece-property-buyers-non-residents/).

A tax representative in Greece has been **optional since 9 September 2024**, if you accept that the tax office serves notices at the contact details you declared ([AADE decision A.1069/2024](https://aade.gr/sites/default/files/2024-05/A1069_2024_FEK.pdf)). Notices are in Greek with short deadlines, so many owners still appoint one ([tax representation in Greece](/guides/fiscal-representation-greece/)).

### AMA registration

Each property gets its own **AMA** (registry number) in AADE's Short-Term Stay Property Registry. Rooms let separately each need their own AMA. The AMA must appear on every listing.

### Safety and operating standards

Since **1 October 2025**, every registered property must meet minimum standards ([Law 5170/2025, art. 3](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5170/2025/arthro/3)):

- civil liability insurance,
- an electrician's declaration confirming a residual current device,
- fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, emergency lighting and exit signs,
- natural light, ventilation and air conditioning in bedrooms,
- a pest control certificate and a first aid kit.

Joint inspections by the tourism ministry and AADE bring fines from €5,000.

### Is Crete affected by the Airbnb registration freeze?

No. New AMA registrations are suspended until 31 December 2026 only in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd municipal districts of Athens and the 1st municipal community of Thessaloniki ([housing ministry](https://stegasi.gov.gr/programs/prosorinoi-periorismoi-stis-vrachychronies-misthoseis/)). The government announced an extension of that freeze to 31 December 2027; the decision had not been issued as of mid-November 2026. Local voices in Chania have called for limits, but so far no rule restricts new registrations anywhere on Crete.

## During the season

### A declaration for every stay

For each booking you file a **short-term stay declaration** in myAADE by the **20th of the month after the guest's departure**. A cancelled booking you still get paid for is declared too. Corrections are possible until 28 February of the following year; then the data is locked and pre-fills your tax return.

### The climate resilience fee

The guest pays a fee per night and per property (or room), which you collect and pass on ([Law 5162/2024, art. 24](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5162/2024/arthro/24)):

| Property | April – October | November – March |
|---|---|---|
| Short-term rental | €8 per night | €2 per night |
| Detached house over 80 m² | €15 per night | €4 per night |

You file a monthly return and pay the fee by the end of the following month ([AADE FAQ, February 2026](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2026-02/FAQs_el_telos_anthektikotitas_stin_klimatiki_krisi_02_02_2026.pdf)). The fee is not rental income and carries no VAT. A late return costs €100 for an individual.

### Platforms report you

Under DAC7, the EU rules that make digital platforms report sellers' income (in Greece, [Law 5047/2023](https://www.aade.gr/en/aeoi-automatic-exchange-information-dac7)), Airbnb, Booking.com and others report hosts' payouts every year, and AADE matches them against your declarations.

## Short-term rental tax in Greece: how the income is taxed

### One or two properties: rental income

With up to two short-term rental properties, an individual is taxed on **income from property**, with no VAT, on this scale for income from 1 January 2026 ([Law 5246/2025](https://kpmg.com/gr/en/insights/2025/11/tax-updates-20112025.html)):

| Annual rental income | Rate |
|---|---|
| up to €12,000 | 15% |
| €12,000.01 – 24,000 | 25% |
| €24,000.01 – 36,000 | 35% |
| above €36,000 | 45% |

There is no tax-free amount, and actual costs are not deductible; a flat **5%** of gross rent is deducted instead.

**Example:** a villa near Rethymno let for 80 nights at €200 brings €16,000. Minus 5%, €15,200 is taxable: €1,800 on the first €12,000 plus €800 on the rest, so **€2,600** of Greek tax.

Co-owners are taxed on their own share, on their own scale.

### Three properties or more: a business

From the third short-term rental property, all your short-term lets become **business income**. You register a business activity with the tax office, charge **13% VAT** on all properties, keep books, report receipts to [myDATA](/guides/mydata-greece-explained/) and file form E3.

## Filing your Greek tax return as a non-resident

Each year with rent you file an income tax return (E1) with **form E2**, listing every property and its rent, between mid-March and **15 July**. The tax is paid in full by 31 July or in instalments starting then. See our guide to the [Greek tax return for foreigners](/guides/greek-tax-return-e1-foreigners/).

If you only use the house yourself, a non-resident has no income tax return to file. You still declare the property in form E9 and pay ENFIA, the annual property tax ([AADE FAQ for Greeks abroad](https://aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-11/FAQs_omogeneis_gr_0.pdf)).

## Tax in your home country

- **Germany:** under the [Germany–Greece treaty](/guides/germany-greece-double-tax-treaty/), rent from Greek property is taxed in Greece and exempt in Germany. Since Greece is an EU country, the exempt rent does not raise the rate on your other income either ([§ 32b(1) sentence 2 no. 3 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32b.html)).
- **UK:** UK residents pay [UK tax on overseas rental income](https://www.gov.uk/tax-foreign-income/rent) through Self Assessment and can claim Foreign Tax Credit Relief for the Greek tax. More in our [UK to Greece guide](/guides/moving-to-greece-from-uk-tax-guide/).

Keep the Greek assessment notice (ekkatharistiko) and proof of payment for your home tax office.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to register my holiday home in Crete before renting it on Airbnb?

Yes. Every property let short-term needs its own number (AMA) in AADE's Short-Term Stay Property Registry, whether you use Airbnb, Booking.com or rent directly. You need a Greek tax number (AFM) and myAADE access to register.

### How is Airbnb income from a holiday home in Greece taxed?

With up to two short-term rental properties, it is rental income on a separate scale: 15% up to €12,000, 25% to €24,000, 35% to €36,000 and 45% above, for income from 1 January 2026. A flat 5% of gross rent is deducted for repairs and maintenance. There is no VAT.

### Is there a ban on new Airbnb registrations in Crete?

No. As of November 2026, the freeze on new registrations covers only central Athens and central Thessaloniki. Properties in Crete can still be registered if they meet the safety standards in force since 1 October 2025.

### Do I have to file a Greek tax return if I only rent my holiday home for a few weeks?

Yes. Any rent from a Greek property is Greek-source income, so you file an income tax return (E1) with form E2 for that year, between mid-March and 15 July. If you only use the house yourself, a non-resident has no income tax return to file, but still files the property declaration (E9) and pays ENFIA.

### Do Germans have to pay German tax on rent from a holiday home in Greece?

No. Under the Germany–Greece tax treaty, rent from Greek property is taxed in Greece and exempt in Germany. As Greece is in the EU, it does not raise the rate on your other income either (§ 32b(1) sentence 2 no. 3 EStG).
