Article 5C halves the income tax on your Greek salary for seven years if you move your tax residence to Greece. For employees, most cases turn on one question: **who employs you**. This guide covers employers, payroll, the public sector, deadlines and job changes. The general rules of the regime are in [how the 50% tax break works](/guides/50-percent-tax-break-article-5c/).

## The short answer

- **What you get:** income tax on only 50% of your Greek employment income for seven tax years. EFKA is paid in full.
- **Who qualifies:** you were not Greek tax resident in 5 of the previous 6 years, you move from an EU/EEA country or one with a tax cooperation agreement, you work for a **Greek legal entity or the Greek permanent establishment of a foreign company**, and you declare you will stay at least two years.
- **Remote employee of a foreign company with no Greek presence:** not eligible as an employee. The options are a Greek employing entity or the self-employed route.
- **Digital Nomad Visa holders:** not eligible at all.
- **No "new position" test** since 28 July 2025. **Public-sector employees** can apply since decision A.1096/2026.
- **Deadline:** start by 2 July, apply by 31 December of that year. Start later, apply by 31 December of the following year.

## Which employers qualify for 5C?

[Article 5C of the Income Tax Code](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4172/2013/arthro/5Γ) covers employment with a Greek legal person or legal entity, or with the permanent establishment of a foreign company in Greece. The [AADE 5C FAQ](https://aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-10/FAQS_5G_KFE.pdf) (2 October 2025, Q2 and Q6) repeats that wording.

### Employee of a Greek company

Any Greek employer qualifies, including a company you own if it pays you a salary.

The employer no longer has to increase its headcount. [Law 5222/2025](https://kpmg.com/gr/en/insights/2025/08/tax-updates-06082025.html) (art. 206(7)) repealed the "new job position" paragraph from 28 July 2025. The repeal also applies to applications pending on that date.

### Employee of a foreign company with a Greek branch

If your employer has a Greek subsidiary or a Greek permanent establishment, you can be hired or transferred there. The Greek entity then runs payroll, declares you in ERGANI and withholds tax.

What about secondments? Under [E.2224/2021](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2021-12/e_2224_2021ada.pdf) (para. 4, written for the now-repealed new-position test), a secondment from a foreign company to a Greek company was treated as Greek employment where the Greek company directs the work and bears the payroll cost.

### Can I get 5C as a remote worker for a foreign company?

Not if your contract is with a company abroad that has no Greek entity or permanent establishment. That is not an employment relationship the law covers. AADE has published nothing that opens the employment route for remote work, including for foreign employers that register with EFKA or run Greek payroll without a Greek establishment.

What the sources support:

- **Employment through a Greek entity.** A move to a Greek group company or branch qualifies. An Employer of Record that is itself a Greek company and employs you under a Greek contract may meet the wording, but there is no AADE guidance on EoR set-ups, so treat it as untested.
- **The self-employed route.** You register as a sole proprietor in Greece, invoice your clients, and apply for 5C on your business profit. Setup is in [how to register as a freelancer in Greece](/guides/freelancer-setup-greece/), the taxes in [freelancer taxes in Greece 2026](/guides/freelancer-taxes-greece-2026/). On this route, 20% withholding on Greek clients' fees is calculated on the full gross fee; the exemption is applied when your annual return is assessed (E.2224/2021, paras 18–20).

**Disguised-employment risk.** Invoicing your former employer as its only client, on the same hours and instructions as before, can be challenged as disguised employment, in Greece or in the company's country. Several clients and genuinely independent terms reduce that risk. Check this before you resign.

Staying a remote employee without 5C? Tax, EFKA and A1 rules for that set-up are in [working in Greece for a foreign employer](/guides/remote-employee-foreign-employer-greece/).

### Can Digital Nomad Visa holders get 5C?

No. The visa forbids work for Greek employers and Greek business activity, which is what 5C requires. See [why the Digital Nomad Visa rules out 5C](/guides/5c-vs-digital-nomad-visa/).

## Can company directors and founders use 5C?

"Employment" is read broadly. Under [E.2224/2021](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2021-12/e_2224_2021ada.pdf) (para. 5), directors and board members of a Greek company are in an employment relationship for 5C purposes, and fees paid to managing partners of an IKE or EPE, or partners of an OE or EE, for their role count as employment income. Founders paying themselves a director's fee from their own Greek company can therefore qualify. Dividends and company profits are not covered.

## Public-sector employees

The exclusion of the public sector sat in the implementing decision A.1087/2021, not in the statute. Joint decision [A.1096/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/52732/a-1096-2026) of the Deputy Finance Minister and the AADE Governor (signed 29 April 2026, FEK B' 2500/4.5.2026, decision number corrected in FEK B' 2672/13.5.2026) deleted it.

People paid under the public-sector pay legislation for civil servants and public officials can now apply. The change covers applications filed from 28 July 2025 and those pending on that date.

## How 5C works on your payslip

Once the approval decision is issued, your employer withholds income tax on 50% of your salary and **none on the other half** (E.2224/2021, para. 11). Only the employer named in the decision applies the reduced withholding (para. 12). Each employer needs its own decision.

Until the decision is issued, normal withholding applies. AADE sets out no payroll refund, so in practice you get the difference back with your annual return.

What does not change:

- **EFKA** is calculated on your full gross salary: 13.37% employee and 21.79% employer in the private sector (rates since 2025), on pay up to the 2026 ceiling of €7,761.94 a month ([e-EFKA circular 4/2026](https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/51989/egkyklios-e-efka-4-2026)).
- **The electronic-spending requirement** is calculated on your total income, including the exempt half (E.2224/2021, para. 21).

### How much does 5C save on a salary?

Gross salary €40,000 in 2026, no children, over 30. After employee EFKA, about €34,650 is taxable on the [2026 scale](https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5246/2025/arthro/3) (9% to €10,000, then 20%, 26%, 34%, 39% and 44% above €60,000).

- **Without 5C:** roughly **€6,750** income tax after the employee tax credit.
- **With 5C:** €17,325 taxable, roughly **€1,700** after the credit.

About €5,000 a year, around €35,000 over seven years. The post-credit figures are approximate; the guidance does not spell out how the credit phases out for 5C cases. For your own numbers, use the [5C tax calculator](/greece/5c-tax-calculator/).

## When to apply: the 2 July rule

The start date of your job decides your first 5C year ([AADE FAQ](https://aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-10/FAQS_5G_KFE.pdf), Q9):

- **Start on or before 2 July:** apply by 31 December of the same year; the regime covers that year. You can still apply during the following year, but the regime then starts only in that year and your first year's salary is taxed in full.
- **Start after 2 July:** apply by 31 December of the following year; the regime starts in that year.

Example: start on 1 August and apply in January, and you are on time. Start on 1 June and wait until January, and you lose a full year of the exemption.

## How to apply and what documents you need

You apply after the move, in myAADE (Tax services → Income → "Application for the special tax regime, Article 5C"), yourself or through an accountant. Under [A.1138/2025](https://aade.gr/sites/default/files/2025-10/a1138ada_0.pdf), which rewrote A.1087/2021:

- **Employment is checked automatically** against ERGANI (for the public sector, the Interior Ministry's HR register, per A.1096/2026). If everything matches, the decision is immediate; otherwise the tax office decides within 60 days.
- **Mandatory fields:** your foreign tax ID and your full last foreign address in Latin characters.
- **Proof of non-residence** only for years Greek records do not already show, typically a tax residence certificate from your previous country (see [tax residency certificates](/guides/greek-tax-residency-certificate/)).
- **Missing documents:** the office gives you 30 days. If the application is rejected for missing documents, you can still file them by 31 March of the following year and it is re-examined.
- **Separate decisions** are issued per employer and per income category.

An ERGANI mismatch (wrong start date or employer tax number, late declaration) sends the application to manual review, so ask HR to check the entry before you apply.

## What happens if you change jobs, lose your job or leave?

The seven years run from your first 5C year and cannot be extended. Within them (A.1138/2025, art. 4; AADE FAQ, Q12–Q16):

- **Change of employer:** you notify AADE through the online application. If you still meet the conditions, a new decision continues the regime for the remaining years.
- **Second job:** you extend the application; the new decision covers only the years left.
- **Job ends:** you keep the status if, within 12 months, you sign a new employment contract or start a sole proprietorship in Greece.
- **No new activity within 12 months, or you move your tax residence abroad:** a cessation decision is issued and you are taxed under the normal rules from that tax year onward.
- **Re-applying:** possible within the original seven-year window. If you stayed Greek tax resident in between, the prior-residence conditions are not re-examined.

Renewing a fixed-term contract with the same employer is not a change of position; the regime continues ([E.2029/2022](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2022-03/e2029_2022ada.pdf)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get the 50% tax break in Greece if I work remotely for a foreign company?

The options are employment through a Greek entity (a Greek Employer of Record is untested, with no AADE guidance) or registering as self-employed in Greece under the business route.

### Does my job have to be a newly created position for Article 5C?

No. Law 5222/2025 (art. 206(7)) repealed the new-position condition with effect from 28 July 2025, including for applications pending on that date. An existing role with a Greek employer qualifies.

### How does Article 5C work on my payslip?

Once the approval decision is issued, the employer named in it withholds income tax on 50% of your salary and none on the other half. EFKA contributions are still calculated on your full gross salary. Months withheld at the normal rate before the decision are settled through your annual return.

Yes. Decision A.1096/2026 (FEK B' 2500/4.5.2026) removed the exclusion of people paid under public-sector pay legislation. It covers applications filed from 28 July 2025 and applications pending on that date.

### Do directors' fees qualify for Article 5C?

### What is the Article 5C deadline for employees?

### What happens to Article 5C if I change jobs or lose my job?
