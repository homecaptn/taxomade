Your accountant hasn't replied in two weeks. Everything you receive is in Greek and nobody explains it. Fines arrive and nobody warned you. If that sounds familiar, you're not stuck: switching accountants in Greece is your decision, it can be done at any time of the year, and in most cases it takes days, not months.

Here's how to do it without leaving a gap in your filings.

## The short answer

- **You can switch at any time**, including in the middle of the year.
- **You control the access.** Authorizations in myAADE are granted and revoked by you, not by your accountant.
- **The handover is the critical part:** filed returns, books, open obligations and deadlines.
- **Timing:** ideally at the end of a month, and not right before a VAT or tax deadline.
- **Duration:** in our experience about a week, once your old accountant has sent your records.

## Signs it's time to switch

Most people switch for the same reasons:

- **Late filings or fines** you only find out about afterwards
- **No answers**, or answers that take days or weeks
- **Language:** everything comes in Greek, nothing is explained
- **Surprise extra fees** on top of the monthly price
- **No guidance:** nobody tells you about deadlines, 5C, VAT rules or e-invoicing until it's too late

One of these on its own might be a bad week. Several of them together usually mean the setup isn't working for you.

## Step 1: Check your contract and your status

Before you announce anything, check two things:

1. **Your contract:** Is there a notice period? Some accountants work with monthly agreements, others with longer terms.
2. **Your current status:** Ask your accountant in writing for an overview of what has been filed, what is still open and which deadlines are coming up.

Doing this first makes the rest of the handover much easier, and it gives your new accountant a clear starting point.

## Step 2: Choose your new accountant

Look beyond the monthly price. Ask what is actually included: VAT returns, myDATA, the annual tax return, e-invoicing, and how quickly they reply. ## Step 3: Give your new accountant access in myAADE

In Greece, your accountant works on your behalf through the tax authority's online system. The authorization comes from you:

1. Log in to [myAADE](https://www.aade.gr/myaade) with your TAXISnet credentials.
2. Go to **Registry & Communication → Authorizations** (Μητρώο & Επικοινωνία → Εξουσιοδοτήσεις).
3. Enter your new accountant's tax number (AFM) and select which services they may handle. You can authorize specific services only and change this later.
4. Your new accountant logs in to their own myAADE account and accepts the authorization. Until then it shows as pending.

You never need to share your password. In the same menu you can revoke your old accountant's authorization; the revocation takes effect immediately. It's good practice to also tell them in writing.

Your new accountant will also check access to the other systems your business uses, for example myDATA, e-EFKA, ERGANI if you have employees, and your invoicing or e-invoicing provider.

## Step 4: Get your records handed over

Ask your old accountant, in writing, to hand over:

| What | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Filed tax and VAT returns with proof of submission | Shows what has been declared and when |
| Books and ledgers | Your new accountant continues from here |
| Open obligations and upcoming deadlines | Nothing gets missed during the switch |
| Payroll and employee records (if any) | Needed for social security and ERGANI |
| Access details for invoicing software or e-invoicing provider | Your invoicing keeps running without interruption |

Under the accountants' code of conduct, your old accountant is expected to hand over your documents to your new accountant with a written handover record. Settle any fees you still owe for work already done first, because unpaid fees can hold up the handover.

## Step 5: Pick the right moment

You can switch any day, but some moments are easier than others:

- **End of a month** keeps the accounting periods clean.
- **Not right before a deadline:** VAT returns, for example, are due by the last working day of the month after the period ends. Switching a few days before that adds unnecessary pressure.
- **Before your annual tax return season** is a good moment if you want your new accountant to handle the full year.

## How long it takes

In our experience, a clean switch takes **about a week** once your records are available. It takes longer if documents arrive slowly or if earlier filings contain errors that need correcting first. That's also the moment many people find out what was missed, which is often the best reason to switch sooner rather than later.

## FAQ

### Can I change my accountant in Greece in the middle of the year?

Yes. You can switch accountants at any time of the year. What matters is a clean handover of your records and filings, ideally at the end of a month and not right before a filing deadline.

### Do I need my old accountant's permission to switch?

No. Authorizations in myAADE are granted and revoked by you, the taxpayer, with your own TAXISnet credentials. Check your contract for a notice period, and settle any fees you still owe, as unpaid fees can delay the handover.

### How do I give my new accountant access in Greece?

You log in to myAADE with your TAXISnet credentials, open Registry & Communication, then Authorizations, enter your new accountant's tax number (AFM) and select the services they may handle. Your accountant then accepts the authorization in their own account. You never need to share your password.

### What should my old accountant hand over?

Copies of all filed tax and VAT returns with proof of submission, your books and ledgers, an overview of open obligations and upcoming deadlines, and, if relevant, payroll and employee records and access details for your invoicing software or e-invoicing provider.

### How long does switching accountants take in Greece?

In our experience, a clean handover usually takes about a week once your records are available. It can take longer if your old accountant is slow to send documents or if past filings need correcting.
